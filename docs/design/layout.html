Owe Yeah: app layout

The target layout for the app, decided in a design session on 1 November 2026. It is a mobile app first: on a wide screen the same layout sits in a centered column about 480 px wide. Each screen below is what to build; anything with a dashed purple outline arrives with the ticket named on it and is not built by the layout tickets. Data is invented: Trip "Croatia 2026", and this device has claimed Ania .

Rules that apply everywhere

  • Three layouts. default for app pages (My Trips, New Trip): a header with the app mark. trip for the Trip's tabs: the Trip header and a bottom tab bar (Expenses · Balances · Trip). form for full-screen forms and detail pages: back or close, a title cut to one line, and a fixed action at the bottom. No tab bar.
  • Routes. /trips/:id (Expenses), /trips/:id/balances , /trips/:id/settings (the "Trip" tab), /trips/:id/expenses/new , /trips/:id/expenses/:expenseId and …/edit . A parent trips/[id].vue loads the Trip once for its children.
  • The Trip link's token is only needed to join. Opening /trips/:id#token stores the token with the Trip on the device, then replaces the URL with /trips/:id . Every request takes the token from the device. A device that does not know the Trip is told to open it with its Trip link.
  • Mobile navigation. Forms and detail pages slide in from the right and out on back (View Transitions, instant where unsupported); switching tabs is instant. Back goes back in history, or to the parent screen when there is none (a deep link). System back closes an open sheet first. Header and tab bar respect safe areas ( viewport-fit=cover ). Each tab keeps its scroll position. Inputs keep Nuxt UI's sizes: 16 px on phones (no iOS zoom), 14 px on wide screens. Amounts use inputmode="decimal" .
  • Floating button only where needed. For now only the Expenses tab has one ("+ Expense").
  • Long text never pushes amounts. Rows and headers keep text on one line and cut it with "…"; amounts never shrink or wrap. Full text wraps only on the detail page and the Trip tab. Status badges sit on the subtitle line, not beside the title. Participant chips and bar labels have a maximum width.
  • Participants have avatars. UAvatar with the initial and a color from a fixed palette of about 8, picked by the Participant's id, so it is the same on every device.
  • Color. Primary emerald, neutral slate. The balance card is green when you're owed, red when you owe, neutral when you're even or have no Claim. Color mode follows the system; the manual toggle lives in the My Trips "⋯" menu.
